About

What I’m doing lately

I’m Peter Zhu (朱磊), an engineer based in Suzhou. At ECARX I spend most days on systems that have to work in production — serving models efficiently, and making search and retrieval reliable when keyword and vector signals disagree.

Outside work I contribute to open-source projects and maintain a few focused repositories of my own. I care about readable code, reproducible benches, and small libraries that fit into existing stacks without ceremony.

If you’re looking for a long list of buzzwords, this isn’t that page. Prefer talking about what shipped and what broke.
